    Documentation & Trade Alignment

    Technical, inspection and trade documents

    The document package is defined by the product, origin, destination and contract. The categories below outline the records that may be requested and reviewed.

    Product specification & certificate of analysis

    The specification sets the agreed product limits and test methods. The certificate of analysis records results for an identified sample or shipment batch.

    Independent Inspection & Verification (SGS / Intertek / BV)

    Independent third-party inspection can be coordinated per shipment when required or agreed. Official reports are shared with the relevant qualified counterparties according to the transaction.

    Trade & EU-Related Documentation

    Reference documentation illustrates the types of records that may be reviewed for applicable import, product and transaction requirements. Final documentation depends on product, origin, destination and shipment structure.

    Water-Soluble & Specialty Fertilizer Documentation

    COA, TDS, MSDS, REACH, test reports and heavy-metals analysis are reviewed by product where applicable. Final documentation depends on the selected supply source and shipment requirements.

    Request Shipment-Specific Documents

    Factory capability & product conformity review

    Request a defined review of manufacturer identity, supply authority, declared production capacity, current availability and the product documents relevant to your order. Reported factory capacity is distinguished from stock or allocation available for a specific shipment.

    Site audits, sampling, laboratory tests and conformity checks can be coordinated with suitable independent providers, subject to an agreed scope, access, availability and cost. Euro Agri coordinates the process; reports and certifications are issued by the responsible provider.

    Request a review scope

    Phosphate documentation by origin

    Request documentation for a specified mine, producer and grade from Egypt, Morocco or Jordan. Origin alone does not establish quality or availability. Manufacturer specifications and representative analyses are distinguished from the certificate of analysis for the actual shipment.

    The requested file may cover total and soluble phosphorus fractions with their test methods, moisture, particle size, cadmium and other relevant impurities, origin evidence and the destination requirements. We do not publish a single guaranteed specification or certificate for an entire country.

    Technical & commercial guidance

    Product guidance: clarify the exact chemical or commercial identity, nutrient declaration, units, test methods and intended use. A specification defines agreed limits; a certificate of analysis records test results for an identified sample or batch. Any standard cited must identify the applicable product, method and edition.

    Commercial guidance: match the grade to the required quantity, packing, delivery point, timing and payment structure. For a farm programme, product selection and application rates need qualified agronomic review using crop, soil and water information.
