    Progressive Supplier & Transaction Verification

    Verification is proportionate to the stage of the transaction, focusing first on commercial feasibility and then on deeper counterparty and shipment checks as required.

    1. Commercial Fit & Availability Product, quantity, current availability, commercial basis and delivery feasibility are checked first.
    2. Entity & Supply Role Review Company identity, trading role, manufacturer/distributor relationship and relevant business evidence are reviewed as appropriate.
    3. Product & Shipment Evidence COA, specifications, stock or allocation evidence and independent inspection are reviewed when required by the transaction.
    4. Transaction & Documentation Alignment Counterparty, payment, commercial documents and shipment requirements are aligned before execution.
    Counterparty identities and shipment-specific documents are shared only when commercially necessary and after the relevant RFQ, qualification and engagement steps have been completed.

    Structured Trade & Engagement Framework

    Euro Agri Trading s.r.o. coordinates sourcing, commercial alignment and transaction workflows under an engagement structure appropriate to each deal, with clear roles, commercial terms and relationship protection.

    Commercial Engagement Terms Governed by written agreement according to the transaction or cooperation scope.
    Payment Structure LC at Sight is preferred where commercially feasible. Other payment structures are reviewed case-by-case according to the transaction and counterparty profile.
    Confidentiality & Client Protection Counterparty details are disclosed only when commercially necessary and after appropriate engagement alignment.
    Documentation Control Product, commercial and shipment documents are reviewed for consistency according to the transaction stage.
    Supplier or buyer identities are not disclosed prematurely. Counterparty information is released only when required for a qualified commercial process and under the applicable engagement framework.
