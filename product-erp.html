    Phosphate materials

    Rock phosphate powder

    Powder and fine-powder options. Grade, origin and allocation confirmed per request.

    Request a quotation
    Listed grade18-34% P₂O₅

    Listed grades identify the sourcing range, not guaranteed nutrient minima. N-P-K expresses nitrogen (N), phosphate as P₂O₅ and potassium as K₂O. Guaranteed values, analytical basis and methods are confirmed in the agreed supplier specification.

    Technical specificationApplications & suitabilityPacking & deliveryDocuments & market requirements

    Technical specification

    ParameterProduct-specific confirmation
    Scientific & commercial identityRock phosphate is a mineral raw material, not a pure chemical compound. Specify total P₂O₅, origin and particle size. BPL is a different reporting basis; it must not be read as percent P₂O₅.
    Grade & declarationConfirm the exact product identity, nutrient minima and test methods in the supplier TDS. The shipment COA reports results against the agreed limits.
    Phosphorus fractionsState total, available and water-soluble P₂O₅ separately, including extraction method and reporting basis. These values are not interchangeable.
    Physical propertiesConfirm moisture, particle-size distribution and product form. For granules, agree strength and fines limits where needed.
    Cadmium & other impuritiesAgree limits and test methods for the destination. Distinguish mg/kg of product from mg/kg of P₂O₅, and state the moisture basis.

    Applications & suitability

    Phosphate raw materials for fertilizer manufacture and formulation. Direct soil application is assessed only where product reactivity, soil conditions and destination rules support it.

    Packing & delivery

    Tell us your preferred packing and handling requirements. Available bag weights, big bags or bulk options, marking, minimum order quantity and delivery timing are confirmed with the selected supply source before quotation.

    Documents & market requirements

    The document set is agreed for the product and destination: technical data sheet (TDS), safety data sheet (SDS) where applicable, and certificate of analysis (COA) for the actual shipment. Representative analyses are identified as such. Inspection by an independent provider may be coordinated when agreed.

    Applicable EU and destination-market requirements are assessed for the selected product and supply route. REACH status, CE marking or any other compliance claim is supported by the relevant product documents before it is made.

    Request specifications

    Send the quantity, destination, preferred packing and delivery window. The product and grade will be carried into your enquiry.
