# Product technical review — 31 October 2026

Scope: all 32 entries in the public product range, generated product pages, catalogue/specialty cards, RFQ product labels and grade links, and both downloadable company PDFs. This is a terminology and declaration review, not supplier qualification, a laboratory analysis, or a certification of conformity.

## Decisions

- Retain GSSP 17% and 19%, GSP 20%, DSP 36%, granular ammophos 10-46-0, MKP 0-52-34 and other existing requested grades. No supplier-wide nutrient minimum, tolerance or availability guarantee was created.
- Expand DSP to double superphosphate. Retain GSP as a supplier-specific commercial label pending the exact manufacturer identity and analytical basis. Do not rename it GSSP 20% or add GRP 20%.
- Distinguish granular ammophos from pure monoammonium phosphate and soluble MAP 12-61-0. Do not apply a pure-compound formula to the commercial blend.
- Express chelate grades as 6% Fe · EDDHA and 15% Zn · EDTA. The figures refer to nutrient mass, not chelating-agent content. The ortho-ortho fraction needs a separate declaration.
- Remove an absolute chloride-free statement and a fixed phosphorus range attached to the low-cadmium label from old translation entries.
- Correct the PDF statement that a COA defines specification limits: the agreed specification defines limits; a COA records sample/lot results. Add the raw-material range to the catalogue and remove undefined ERP from multi-origin powder descriptions.
- Existing formulae for DAP, MKP, UP, urea, potassium nitrate, magnesium nitrate hexahydrate, dolomite, calcium carbonate, phosphoric acid and potassium chloride were checked and retained.
- Isolate chemical notation and nutrient grades within Arabic technical prose to preserve their left-to-right reading order.

## Evidence and limits

They are technical references only and do **not** assert a supply relationship, authorize use of a manufacturer's specification for another producer, or establish current stock. Source-specific product documents were also reviewed privately; confidential supplier files and correspondence are not reproduced in this repository.

5. [IFFCO urea phosphate](https://www.iffco.in/en/urea-phosphate): 17-44-0 product identity. Product-specific application rates are not copied into this site.
6. [Mosaic/IPNI single superphosphate](https://www.cropnutrition.com/resource-library/single-superphosphate/): calcium phosphate and calcium sulfate components.
7. [Mosaic/IPNI triple superphosphate](https://www.cropnutrition.com/resource-library/triple-superphosphate/): monocalcium-phosphate-based fertilizer identity.
8. [Mosaic DAP technical sheet](https://www.cropnutrition.com/resource-library/diammonium-phosphate-dap-data-sheet/): DAP grade 18-46-0 and available-phosphate declaration.
9. [IFA technical conference paper on SP-36](https://www.fertilizer.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/1998_ifa_marrakech_husein.pdf) and [Petrokimia Gresik SP-36](https://petrokimia-gresik.com/product/pupuk-sp-36): double-superphosphate terminology and the distinction between total and different extractable phosphorus fractions. 10. [Yara soluble fertilizer range](https://www.yara.co.uk/crop-nutrition/fertiliser/soluble/): separate Fe-EDDHA and Zn-EDTA formulations and ortho-ortho declarations.

## Product-by-product record

| Entry | Listed grade | Identity and declaration review |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rock phosphate powder | 18-34% P₂O₅ | Rock phosphate is a mineral raw material, not a pure chemical compound. Specify total P₂O₅, origin and particle size. BPL is a different reporting basis; it must not be read as percent P₂O₅. |
| Granular rock phosphate / GRP | Grade on request | Granular rock phosphate (GRP) describes the physical form of phosphate rock. Granulation alone does not establish water solubility or make the material superphosphate. |
| GRP + sulfur / additives | Composition on request | A phosphate-rock formulation with sulfur and/or additives. Declare P₂O₅ and sulfur separately, identify elemental sulfur or sulfate, and state whether sulfur is reported as S or SO₃. |
| Low-cadmium phosphate grades | Lot-specific analysis | Low cadmium is an analytical requirement, not a distinct chemical compound or a fixed phosphorus grade. State the numerical Cd limit, method, moisture basis and units; mg/kg of product and mg/kg of P₂O₅ are not equivalent. |
| Granular single superphosphate / GSSP | 17% P₂O₅ | Granular single superphosphate (GSSP): a phosphate fertilizer containing monocalcium phosphate and calcium sulfate. The listed 17% P₂O₅ requires a stated extraction and reporting basis; calcium and sulfur contents are declared separately. |
| Granular single superphosphate / GSSP | 19% P₂O₅ | Granular single superphosphate (GSSP): a phosphate fertilizer containing monocalcium phosphate and calcium sulfate. The listed 19% P₂O₅ requires a stated extraction and reporting basis; it is a separate grade from GSSP 17% and GSP 20%. |
| GSP | 20% P₂O₅ | GSP 20% is retained as a commercial designation for a granular phosphatic fertilizer. The abbreviation and percentage alone do not establish its chemical composition or P₂O₅ solubility. Confirm the manufacturer's full product identity before quotation. |
| Double superphosphate / DSP | 36% P₂O₅ | Double superphosphate (DSP), listed at 36% P₂O₅. Confirm the exact manufacturer specification and distinguish total, extractable and water-soluble P₂O₅. DSP fertilizer must not be confused with disodium phosphate, which may use the same abbreviation in chemical trade. |
| Triple superphosphate / TSP | 0-46-0 | Triple superphosphate (TSP) is a concentrated phosphate fertilizer based mainly on monocalcium phosphate. The listed 0-46-0 is an N-P₂O₅-K₂O grade. It is not trisodium phosphate, another chemical sometimes abbreviated TSP. |
| Diammonium phosphate / DAP | 18-46-0 | Diammonium hydrogen phosphate, commonly diammonium phosphate (DAP), (NH₄)₂HPO₄. The listed 18-46-0 refers to N, P₂O₅ and K₂O; nitrogen is ammoniacal. The P₂O₅ extraction basis belongs in the product specification. |
| MAP / Granular ammophos | 10-46-0 | Granular ammophos marketed as MAP grade 10-46-0 is an ammonium-phosphate fertilizer. It may contain both NH₄H₂PO₄ and (NH₄)₂HPO₄; do not describe the commercial material as pure monoammonium phosphate or substitute a soluble MAP 12-61-0 specification. |
| Granular NPK | Various formulas | A granular fertilizer supplying nitrogen, phosphorus and potassium. State the N-P₂O₅-K₂O grade and whether the product is a manufactured compound or a physical bulk blend; the grade alone does not establish the manufacturing route. |
| Granular urea | 46% N | Urea, also called carbamide, CO(NH₂)₂. The listed 46% is nitrogen as N, not urea purity. Nitrogen is in the urea form; biuret, moisture and particle-size limits require separate declarations. |
| Monopotassium phosphate / MKP | 0-52-34 | Potassium dihydrogen phosphate, commonly monopotassium phosphate (MKP), KH₂PO₄. The trade grade 0-52-34 is not a universal guarantee of minimum 52% P₂O₅ and 34% K₂O. State the agreed minima, moisture basis and chloride limit from the selected TDS. |
| Urea phosphate / UP | 17-44-0 | Urea phosphate (UP), CO(NH₂)₂·H₃PO₄, is the urea-phosphoric acid adduct, not monoammonium phosphate. The listed 17-44-0 expresses N-P₂O₅-K₂O. Specify nitrogen form, water-soluble P₂O₅ and the concentration and temperature of any pH measurement. |
| Potassium nitrate / NOP | 13-0-46 | Potassium nitrate (KNO₃), also nitrate of potash (NOP). The listed 13-0-46 expresses N-P₂O₅-K₂O, with nitrogen in nitrate form. NOP is different from potassium chloride (MOP); a product-specific chloride limit is still required. |
| Magnesium nitrate hexahydrate | Mg + N | Magnesium nitrate hexahydrate, Mg(NO₃)₂·6H₂O. The six waters of crystallisation are part of the chemical identity. Magnesium as Mg and magnesium as MgO are different reporting bases; do not transfer an anhydrous-salt specification to this material. |
| Water-soluble NPK + TE | 20-20-20 + TE | Water-soluble NPK formulation with a listed N-P₂O₅-K₂O grade of 20-20-20. TE means trace elements; it does not identify which elements, their concentrations or their chelated fractions. These require a separate declaration. |
| Water-soluble NPK + TE | 10-52-10 + TE | Water-soluble NPK formulation with a listed N-P₂O₅-K₂O grade of 10-52-10. TE means trace elements; specify each element and form. This formulation is not MAP or MKP, even though it also has a high phosphate grade. |
| Water-soluble NPK + TE | 15-5-30 + TE | Water-soluble NPK formulation with a listed N-P₂O₅-K₂O grade of 15-5-30. Declare trace elements individually and identify the potassium source where chloride sensitivity matters. The grade does not establish chloride content. |
| Iron chelate | 6% Fe · EDDHA | Iron chelated with EDDHA, listed at 6% Fe by mass. The percentage refers to iron, not the amount of EDDHA. Declare water-soluble iron, chelated iron and iron chelated by the ortho-ortho isomer separately, with the basis for each percentage. |
| Zinc chelate | 15% Zn · EDTA | Zinc chelated with EDTA, listed at 15% Zn by mass. The percentage refers to zinc, not EDTA. Verify total, water-soluble and chelated zinc and the declared pH stability range for the actual formulation. |
| Manganese | Mn | Manganese nutrition products: Mn identifies the nutrient, not the chemical product. Specify the salt or chelate, hydration where relevant, and manganese content as elemental Mn before selecting a grade. |
| Boron | B | Boron nutrition products: B is an elemental nutrient declaration. Boric acid, borate salts and liquid formulations are different products; identify the actual compound and declare boron content as B. |
| Copper | Cu | Copper nutrition products: specify the actual salt or chelate and declare elemental Cu. Chemical form, hydration and solubility must be identified; the symbol Cu alone is not a fertilizer specification. |
| Molybdenum | Mo | Molybdenum nutrition products: specify the actual molybdate salt or formulation and declare elemental Mo. Counter-ion, hydration and concentration depend on the selected material. |
| Selected specialty formulations | Requirement-led | A sourcing category, not a single compound or standard formula. Identify the intended function, ingredients and nutrient forms before assigning a product name, specification or application claim. |
| Dolomite | Specification on request | Dolomite is a calcium-magnesium carbonate mineral with ideal formula CaMg(CO₃)₂. Natural rock may contain other minerals. CaO and MgO analytical equivalents do not mean the material is quicklime or calcined dolomite. |
| Calcium carbonate | Specification on request | Calcium carbonate, CaCO₃. Distinguish the compound assay from elemental Ca or CaO equivalent, and identify ground or precipitated material where relevant. A chemical formula does not establish fineness, neutralising value or agricultural suitability. |
| Phosphoric acid | Specification on request | Orthophosphoric acid, H₃PO₄. Concentration as H₃PO₄ is different from P₂O₅ equivalent. State mass percent and acid grade; density, solids, fluoride and relevant metals require product-specific limits and test conditions. |
| Potassium chloride / MOP | Specification on request | Potassium chloride, KCl, commonly muriate of potash (MOP) in fertilizer trade. K₂O equivalent is a nutrient reporting convention, not KCl purity. This material contains chloride by composition; it is not potassium nitrate (NOP) or potassium sulfate (SOP). |
| Fertilizer additives & anti-caking agents | Specification on request | Fertilizer additives and anti-caking agents are product-specific formulations, not a single chemical or nutrient grade. Define composition, carrier, compatible fertilizer, application conditions and measurable performance; no universal dosage or effectiveness is implied. |

## Still required before a binding technical offer

Manufacturer identity and current TDS; exact nutrient minima, tolerances and test methods; total/extractable/water-soluble phosphorus basis; moisture/dry basis; impurity limits and correct denominator; actual grade and intended use; shipment-specific evidence; current supply confirmation. Natural minerals, specialty formulations and additives cannot receive a fabricated universal composition. Incomplete or contradictory supplier values require clarification from the issuer.
