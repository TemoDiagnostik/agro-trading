    Business Role

    Euro Agri Trading s.r.o. provides fertilizer sourcing and trade coordination services. Its commercial role and contractual responsibilities are defined for each transaction; depending on the agreed structure, it may act as a sourcing partner, introducer, intermediary or trade coordinator. It may also coordinate transaction-related logistics through independent providers. References to logistics or transport coordination describe facilitation through independent providers. Euro Agri Trading does not itself perform carriage, operate trucks or vessels, or represent a transport fleet or shipping line.
