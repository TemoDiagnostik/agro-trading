    Shipping & Transport Coordination

    Euro Agri Trading supports fertilizer transaction execution by coordinating suitable road and maritime transport requirements through independent logistics and transport providers. Each solution is assessed case by case according to the cargo, packing, quantity, route, Incoterm, delivery window and operational availability.

    European Road Freight Coordination

    Coordination of LTL and FTL road freight across the Czech Republic and Europe, including palletised and Big Bag cargo. Specialised or heavy transport is assessed where required.

    Maritime Freight & Port Coordination

    Coordination support for suitable maritime freight, port and terminal requirements. Ship-to-Ship (STS) transfer or lightering may be assessed through specialist independent providers where commercially and operationally appropriate.

    Shipment Planning & Delivery Coordination

    Alignment of loading points, routes, packing, shipment milestones, delivery requirements and communication between the supplier, buyer and selected independent logistics provider.

    References to logistics or transport coordination describe facilitation through independent providers. Euro Agri Trading does not itself perform carriage, operate trucks or vessels, or represent a transport fleet or shipping line.

    About Euro Agri Trading

    Euro Agri Trading s.r.o. is a Czech Republic-registered fertilizer sourcing and trade coordination company serving importers, distributors and industrial buyers through a multi-origin supply network.

    Our commercial network includes more than 60 independent partners worldwide. Each supply source is assessed for the specific transaction.

    We assess product availability and commercial feasibility, compare supplier offers and coordinate the documents and logistics needed for each transaction. Price, specification, delivery route and payment terms are reviewed together.

    Verification develops with the transaction: product documentation and supplier authority are reviewed before commitments, with independent inspection coordinated when required or agreed. Unresolved material concerns prevent a transaction from progressing.

    Transaction structure is confirmed case-by-case. Euro Agri Trading may act as a sourcing partner, introducer, intermediary or trade coordinator, with contracting roles and commercial responsibilities defined according to each transaction.
