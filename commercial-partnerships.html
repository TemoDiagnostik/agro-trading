    Independent commercial cooperation

    Join Our Independent Commercial Network

    We welcome experienced fertilizer business developers and commercial introducers who bring credible market knowledge and established buyer relationships.

    Independent Fertilizer Business Development Partner / Commercial Introducer
    Apply as a Commercial Partner
    Cooperation model
    • Independent and non-exclusive
    • Results-based commission only
    • Commission on executed and paid transactions
    • Written opportunity and relationship protection

    This is an independent commercial cooperation opportunity, not salaried employment.

    About the opportunity

    Commercial reach backed by structured execution

    Euro Agri Trading connects qualified European demand with multi-origin fertilizer supply channels. We coordinate RFQs, specifications, commercial offers, documentation, inspection, logistics and shipping arrangements according to each transaction.

    Who we are looking for

    Market professionals who can develop executable opportunities

    • Proven experience in fertilizers, agricultural inputs or related commodity trade.
    • Established relationships with qualified buyers or supply channels in your target markets.
    • Practical understanding of RFQs, Incoterms, payment terms and international trade.
    • Ability to develop qualified, verifiable opportunities rather than circulate unsubstantiated inquiries.
    • Professional written English; additional European languages are an advantage.

    Protection & commercial structure

    Clear terms before protected details are exchanged

    Commercial responsibilities and protections are documented before a qualified opportunity advances.

    • Commission terms are agreed in writing for each opportunity or defined cooperation scope.
    • Introduced relationships may be protected through confidentiality, non-circumvention and repeat-order provisions.
    • Commission entitlement is linked to transactions that are executed and paid.
    • Applicants are not asked to disclose client names during the initial application stage.
